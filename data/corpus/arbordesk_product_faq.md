# ArborDesk Product FAQ (Support Knowledge Base)

ArborDesk is the help desk and ticketing software sold by Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company and product used for research purposes). This FAQ is used by the Customer Success team.

## Plans and pricing

ArborDesk is available in three plans:

- Starter: 9 EUR per agent per month, for teams of up to 5 agents.
- Professional: 29 EUR per agent per month, with no limit on the number of agents.
- Enterprise: custom pricing agreed with the sales team.

Annual billing gives a 15% discount compared to monthly billing. A free trial of 14 days is available for the Professional plan and does not require a credit card.

## Availability and hosting

The Professional plan includes an uptime commitment of 99.5% per month. The Enterprise plan includes 99.9% uptime per month with service credits if the commitment is missed. All customer data is hosted in EU data centres in Frankfurt, Germany.

## Features and limits

- Single sign-on (SSO) through SAML is available only on the Enterprise plan.
- The API rate limit is 600 requests per minute per account on the Professional plan and 1,200 requests per minute on the Enterprise plan.
- The maximum attachment size per ticket is 25 MB.
- Built-in integrations are available for Slack, Microsoft Teams, Jira and email.

## Support hours

- Starter: email support, 9:00 to 17:00 CET on business days.
- Professional: email and live chat support, 8:00 to 20:00 CET on business days.
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone support with a named customer success manager.

## Cancellation and data export

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time; the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Customers can export their data in CSV or JSON format at any time. After cancellation, customer data is permanently deleted 60 days after the end of the subscription.
