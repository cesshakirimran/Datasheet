# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: People & Culture team. Version 2.0.

## Hybrid working model

Arbor Systems follows a hybrid working model. Employees work from the office at least 2 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are the company-wide anchor days on which teams are expected to be in the office together. The company has two offices: the headquarters in Győr and a smaller office in Budapest.

Employees become eligible for hybrid work after successfully completing their 3-month probation period. During probation, employees work from the office at least 4 days per week.

Desks are booked through the DeskFinder app no later than 18:00 on the previous working day.

## Fully remote work

Fully remote work is an exception and requires written approval from the relevant Vice President. Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 6 months.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time (CET). During core hours employees must be reachable on Microsoft Teams. Outside core hours employees may organise their working time flexibly, provided they work their contracted hours.

## Home office allowances

Each eligible employee receives a one-time home office equipment budget of 400 EUR, which can be spent on a chair, monitor, keyboard or similar equipment. Purchases are reimbursed through the Expensly expense tool. In addition, employees working in the hybrid model receive a monthly internet allowance of 30 EUR, paid with the salary.

## Working from another country

Employees may work temporarily from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Each request must be approved by HR at least 15 calendar days before the planned start, because of tax and social security rules. Working from a country under international sanctions is not allowed.

## Security when working remotely

When working from any network that is not the company network, employees must connect through the ArborConnect VPN. Using public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited. Company laptops must not be left unattended in public places, and screens must be locked whenever the employee steps away.
