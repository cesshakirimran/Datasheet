# Data Retention and Privacy Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: Data Protection Officer. Version 2.3.

## Purpose

This policy defines how long Arbor Systems keeps different categories of records and how personal data requests are handled. Records must be securely deleted at the end of their retention period unless a legal hold applies.

## Retention periods

| Record type | Retention period |
| --- | --- |
| Customer contract records | 8 years after the contract ends |
| Invoices and accounting records | 8 years |
| Employee personnel files | 5 years after employment ends |
| Job applicant data | 6 months after the recruitment process closes |
| Job applicant data with talent-pool consent | 2 years |
| Customer support tickets | 3 years after the ticket is closed |
| CCTV footage | 30 days |
| Application logs | 90 days |
| Security logs | 1 year |

Backups expire automatically after 35 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 35 days.

## Data subject requests

Requests from individuals to access, correct or delete their personal data (data subject requests) must be forwarded to the Data Protection Officer on the day they are received. Arbor Systems responds to data subject requests within 30 days.

## Personal data breaches

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer immediately. Where required, the supervisory authority is notified within 72 hours of the company becoming aware of the breach.

## Legal holds

When litigation is expected, the Legal team can place a legal hold on records. Records under a legal hold must not be deleted, even if their retention period has ended, until the Legal team releases the hold.
