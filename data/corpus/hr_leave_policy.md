# Annual Leave and Absence Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: People & Culture team. Version 3.2.

## Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Employees with 5 years of continuous service receive 2 additional days (27 days in total). Employees with 10 years of continuous service receive 4 additional days (29 days in total). Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro rata basis according to their contracted hours.

## Carrying over unused leave

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of the following year; any carried-over days not taken by that date are forfeited. Leave cannot be exchanged for payment except when employment ends.

## Requesting leave

All leave is requested through the PeopleHub HR portal. Requests for 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance. Requests for shorter periods must be submitted at least 2 working days in advance. The line manager approves or rejects a request within 3 working days. Leave of more than 15 consecutive working days requires additional approval from the department director.

Members of the Finance team cannot take annual leave during the first 5 working days of January, because of the year-end closing process.

## Sickness absence

Employees who are ill must notify their line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Employees may self-certify sickness for up to 3 consecutive calendar days. From the 4th day of absence, a medical certificate is required and must be uploaded to PeopleHub. Arbor Systems pays up to 15 days of sick leave per calendar year at full salary; after that, statutory sick pay applies.

## Other paid leave

- Bereavement leave: 3 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling) and 1 paid day for an extended family member.
- Volunteering leave: 2 paid days per calendar year for volunteering with a registered charity.
- Moving house: 1 paid day per calendar year.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the People & Culture team through the PeopleHub help desk.
