# Information Security Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: Information Security team. Version 5.0.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected. All employees receive a company-provided 1Password account and must store work passwords there.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems. System administrators and Finance team members must use a hardware security key (YubiKey) as their second factor.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Software may only be installed from the Company Portal; installing software from other sources requires approval from the IT Service Desk.

USB storage devices are blocked by default. An exception can be requested through an IT ticket and requires line manager approval. Restricted data must never be copied to USB storage, even when an exception is granted.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk (internal extension 4400) within 1 hour of discovering the loss.

## Data classification

Company information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data includes customer personal data, credentials and encryption keys. Confidential and Restricted data must never be sent to personal email accounts.

## Phishing and incident reporting

Suspicious emails must be reported using the Report Phish button in Outlook. If the button is not available, the email can be forwarded to the Information Security team mailbox. Employees must never enter their credentials on a page reached through a link in an unexpected email.

## Security awareness training

New employees complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining. All employees repeat the training annually, and the annual training must be completed by 30 November each year.

## Generative AI tools

Only the approved internal assistant, Arbor Assistant, may be used with Internal data. Confidential and Restricted data must never be entered into public generative AI tools.
