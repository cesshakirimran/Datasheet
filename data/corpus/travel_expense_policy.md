# Travel and Expense Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: Finance department. Version 4.1.

## Submitting expenses

All business expenses are submitted through the Expensly expense tool within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Every claim must include an itemised receipt. If a receipt is lost, the employee may submit a missing receipt declaration for amounts up to 25 EUR; a maximum of 3 missing receipt declarations are accepted per employee per calendar year.

Approved expenses are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll if the claim is approved by the 20th day of the month. Claims approved after the 20th are paid with the following payroll.

## Pre-approval of travel

Business trips with an estimated total cost above 500 EUR require pre-approval from the line manager in Expensly before any booking is made.

## Flights

Economy class must be booked for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO). Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible.

## Accommodation

The hotel cost limit is 150 EUR per night in standard locations. In high-cost cities (London, Paris, New York and Zurich) the limit is 220 EUR per night. Amounts above the limit are not reimbursed unless approved in advance by the line manager.

## Meals and daily allowance

The meal per diem is 50 EUR per day for domestic travel and 70 EUR per day for international travel. Alcoholic drinks are not reimbursed, except as part of approved client entertainment.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment is limited to 80 EUR per person. The claim must list the names and companies of all attendees and the business purpose of the meeting.

## Private car use

Employees who use their private car for business travel are reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre. Parking fees and motorway tolls are reimbursed separately with receipts.

## Corporate credit cards

Employees who travel for business more than 4 times per year may request a corporate credit card. Corporate cards must not be used for personal expenses.
