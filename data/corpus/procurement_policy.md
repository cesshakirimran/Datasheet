# Procurement Policy

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: Finance department. Version 3.0.

## Approval limits

| Purchase value (excluding VAT) | Required approval | Quotes required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1,000 EUR | Line manager | None |
| 1,000 to 10,000 EUR | Department head and Finance | 2 quotes |
| 10,000 to 50,000 EUR | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | 3 quotes |
| Over 50,000 EUR | Chief Executive Officer (CEO) | Formal tender |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid an approval limit is a breach of this policy.

## Purchase orders

All purchases are made through purchase orders created in the ProcureNow system. Supplier invoices that do not include a valid purchase order number are rejected by Accounts Payable. The standard payment term for suppliers is 30 days from the invoice date.

## New suppliers

Every new supplier that will process company data must complete the vendor security questionnaire before a contract is signed. If the supplier will process personal data, a data processing agreement (DPA) must also be signed.

All software and SaaS purchases must be reviewed by the Information Security team, regardless of the purchase value.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees involved in selecting a supplier must declare any personal or financial relationship with that supplier before the selection process starts. Employees must not accept gifts from suppliers worth more than 50 EUR.
