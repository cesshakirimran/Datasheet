# New Employee Onboarding Guide

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: People & Culture team. Version 6.0.

## Your first day

On your first day, arrive at 9:00 at the reception of the Győr headquarters. Bring a photo ID and your bank account details for payroll. Your line manager will meet you at reception.

Your laptop is prepared before your first day. New employees can choose between a MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad.

## Your onboarding buddy

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from another team for the first 3 months. The buddy helps with practical questions and introduces the new employee to colleagues.

## Probation period

The probation period lasts 3 months. A check-in meeting with the line manager takes place on day 30, and a probation review meeting takes place at the end of the third month.

## Mandatory training

During the first 2 weeks, every new employee completes three mandatory trainings: security awareness, data protection, and the code of conduct. Security awareness training must in any case be completed within 30 days of joining.

## Benefits

New employees enrol in benefits within 30 days of their start date through PeopleHub. The cafeteria benefit budget is 450,000 HUF per year and can be used for the SZÉP card, health fund contributions, or a public transport pass.

## Engineering onboarding

New engineers request access to code repositories and cloud environments through the IT portal. The goal is that every new engineer opens their first pull request within their first week.

## Leaving the company

When an employee leaves Arbor Systems, all company equipment (laptop, security key, access card) must be returned within 5 working days after the last working day.
