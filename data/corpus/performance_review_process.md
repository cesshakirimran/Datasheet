# Performance Review Process

Arbor Systems Ltd. (fictional company used for research purposes). Owner: People & Culture team. Version 2.4.

## Annual cycle

The performance year runs from January to December. The review cycle has four steps:

1. Self-assessment: employees complete a self-assessment in PeopleHub during November.
2. Manager review: line managers write their reviews during December.
3. Calibration: department leaders calibrate ratings across teams in January.
4. Communication: final ratings and outcomes are communicated to employees by 15 February.

A mid-year check-in conversation between the employee and the line manager takes place in June.

## Rating scale

Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 = below expectations, 2 = partially meets expectations, 3 = meets expectations, 4 = exceeds expectations, 5 = exceptional.

## 360-degree feedback

As part of the annual review, each employee requests feedback from at least 3 peers. Peer feedback is visible to the line manager and is shared with the employee in summarised form.

## Salary increases and bonuses

Salary increases resulting from the review take effect on 1 April. Employees are eligible for an annual bonus if they joined the company on or before 1 October of the performance year. The target bonus is 10% of base salary for individual contributors and 15% of base salary for people managers. The actual bonus depends on the performance rating and company results.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided during the January calibration. To be considered for promotion, an employee must have spent at least 12 months in their current role.

## Performance improvement plans

Employees rated 1 may be placed on a performance improvement plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 days and includes clear goals, weekly check-ins with the line manager, and a final review.
